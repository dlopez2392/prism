# Security policy

Prism handles people's financial data, so we take reports of security problems seriously and thank everyone who makes one.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report it privately in either of two ways:

- on GitHub: this repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**; or
- by email to **privacy@bis-rgv.com**, with "Security" in the subject line.

Please include:

- what you found, and where (a URL, an endpoint, or a file in this repository);
- the steps to reproduce it; and
- what an attacker could do with it.

Please don't open a public GitHub issue for a security problem. We aim to acknowledge a report within three business days and to keep you updated until it's fixed. If you'd like credit, tell us how you'd like to be named.

## Please don't

- access, change or delete anyone else's data (use your own account, or the sandbox and demo household);
- run tests that degrade Prism for other people, such as denial-of-service or high-volume automated scanning; or
- use social engineering or phishing against BIS or Prism's users.

We won't pursue or support legal action against anyone who reports a vulnerability in good faith and within these limits.

## Scope

- In scope: `prism.bis-rgv.com` and the code in this repository.
- Out of scope: third-party services Prism relies on (Plaid, Coinbase, Supabase, Vercel, Resend). Report problems in those to their own security teams.
